About Us

Welcome to Cinderella

At Cinderella, we believe every plushie has the power to bring comfort, spark imagination, and create lasting memories. From bedtime companions to thoughtful gifts, our plush friends are designed to make people of all ages smile.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create high-quality, thoughtfully designed plush toys that inspire joy, comfort, and connection.

Our Vision

We envision a world where everyone has a source of comfort they can hold close, bringing warmth, happiness, and unforgettable memories into everyday life.

Our Story

Cinderella was founded in 2021 by three passionate friends who shared a love for adorable plushies and wanted to spread happiness through comforting, high-quality companions.

Our Values

  • Quality First: Soft, safe, and durable plushies made to last.
  • Creativity: Every plushie has its own personality and unique charm.
  • Kindness: Bringing comfort and happiness to people of all ages.
  • Sustainability: Using environmentally friendly materials whenever possible.

Thank you for being part of the Cinderella family. Whether you're shopping for your first plush friend or adding to a growing collection, we're honored to help create moments of comfort, happiness, and adventure. We look forward to bringing smiles to you and your loved ones for many years to come.
